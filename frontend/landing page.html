Core Capabilities

Intelligent Road Analysis

A complete AI pipeline from detection to repair prioritisation, built for Indian roads.

🎯

YOLOv11 Detection

State-of-the-art object detection trained on custom Indian road datasets identifies potholes with high precision and recall.

📊

RICE Scoring Model

Prioritise repairs using Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort scoring — data-driven decisions for municipal budgets.

🗺️

Risk Zone Mapping

Interactive Bengaluru map with risk zone overlays and live detection markers. Visualise infrastructure health at a glance.

⚡

Severity Assessment Engine

Automatic severity scoring from 1–5, repair type recommendation, cost estimation in INR, and priority classification — all in real-time after each scan.

📷

Image & Video Input

Upload road images or video clips. Frame scrubbing for videos lets you pinpoint the exact moment for analysis.

📈

Model Analytics

Live dashboard with mAP, precision, recall, F1 score, and confusion matrix — full training transparency.

💰

Cost Estimation

Automated repair cost estimation in Indian Rupees based on severity, area coverage, and repair type classification.

Process

How It Works

From upload to actionable repair report in seconds.

01

Upload

Drop a road image or video clip. Supports JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4 — up to 64 MB.

02

Detect

YOLOv11 runs inference in <50ms, drawing bounding boxes with confidence scores.

03

Assess

Severity engine calculates damage level, repair type, cost estimate, and RICE priority score.

04

Prioritise

Results plotted on the Bengaluru risk map. Repairs ranked by urgency for municipal action.
